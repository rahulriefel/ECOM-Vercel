Rahul Mishra

Rahul Mishra is the founder of EcommOcean, a New Delhi agency that builds websites, apps and online stores and manages marketplace accounts for brands selling across India and the US.

He works hands-on with brands on marketplace management across 16 channels — from Amazon and Flipkart to Walmart, Wayfair and Blinkit — alongside e-commerce, website and app development and growth marketing. The focus is always the same: practical work measured against orders and profit, not vanity metrics.

He writes the EcommOcean blog, sharing no-hype, practical guidance on selling on marketplaces, building D2C stores, and growing e-commerce brands in India and abroad.
