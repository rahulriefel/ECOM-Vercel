How selling on Wayfair works

Wayfair is a US home and furniture specialist that often works on a wholesale or dropship model with detailed product-data requirements. It is built for considered, big-ticket home purchases.

Rich attributes, accurate dimensions and strong imagery drive discovery, and CastleGate logistics can speed delivery for large items. Class-leading content matters more here than on general marketplaces.

Who Wayfair suits

Furniture, decor, home-improvement and large-format home brands targeting US shoppers, with the catalogue depth Wayfair's model demands.

Wayfair seller FAQs

How does Wayfair's selling model work?

Wayfair often buys wholesale or dropships rather than a classic marketplace listing model. We help you structure the arrangement and meet its data and logistics standards.

What are the catalogue requirements?

Wayfair needs rich, accurate attributes, dimensions and strong imagery for big-ticket home goods. We build the catalogue to those standards so products are discoverable.

How is logistics handled?

Delivery of large items can run through CastleGate or your own logistics. We help you choose and manage the option that protects margin and delivery speed.
