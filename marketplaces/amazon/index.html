Amazon FAQs

Do you work with both Amazon India and Amazon US?

Yes. We manage Amazon.in for domestic sellers and Amazon US and other global marketplaces for cross-border and US-based brands, including Seller and Vendor Central.

Can you recover a suspended or blocked Amazon account?

In many cases, yes. We diagnose the root cause, draft a Plan of Action and manage the appeal. Outcomes depend on the violation and Amazon's review — we're transparent about what's realistic before we start.

Do I keep control of my Amazon account?

Always. Your Seller/Vendor Central account and data stay 100% yours; we operate inside them with full transparency and hand over clean reporting.
