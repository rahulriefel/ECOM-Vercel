How selling on JioMart works

JioMart is Reliance's fast-growing marketplace spanning groceries, FMCG and general merchandise, with deep reach across India including smaller towns and cities.

Competitive pricing, complete catalogues and consistent availability drive sales across its expanding network. Reliable operations keep your products in stock and selling through in a high-velocity environment.

Who JioMart suits

Grocery, FMCG, household and general-merchandise brands that want broad Indian reach, including tier-2 and tier-3 cities beyond the metros.

JioMart seller FAQs

Which categories sell well on JioMart?

JioMart is strong in groceries, FMCG, household and everyday general merchandise, and it is expanding into more categories. We advise on where your products fit best.

How long does onboarding take?

Most brands launch within a few weeks once catalogue and pricing are ready. We manage setup, item creation and compliance end to end.

How is fulfilment handled?

Depending on the arrangement, orders may ship through JioMart's network or your own logistics. We set up and manage whichever keeps delivery reliable and costs sensible.
