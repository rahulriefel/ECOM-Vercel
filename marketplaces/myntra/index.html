How selling on Myntra works

Myntra is India's fashion-first marketplace, where a large share of the country shops for apparel, footwear and accessories. Catalogue quality and model imagery are effectively non-negotiable.

Sales are driven by trend-aligned merchandising, accurate sizing that reduces returns, and competitive launch pricing that earns early reviews and rank. Presentation carries more weight here than on a generic marketplace.

Who Myntra suits

Fashion, footwear, accessory and lifestyle brands targeting India's style-conscious shoppers, with catalogues that are ready for high-quality, often model-based imagery.

Myntra seller FAQs

What are Myntra's catalogue requirements?

Myntra expects clean, high-quality images, often on models, with multiple angles and detail shots. We handle cataloguing to those standards as part of onboarding.

How do you reduce fashion returns?

Fit is the top return driver, so we use accurate, product-specific size charts and honest descriptions, and track return reasons to fix problem SKUs.

How long does onboarding take?

Most fashion brands launch within a few weeks, depending on catalogue readiness. Getting production-ready imagery is usually the step that sets the timeline.
