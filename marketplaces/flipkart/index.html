How selling on Flipkart works

Flipkart is one of India's largest marketplaces. You list your catalogue, Flipkart charges a category commission plus fixed and shipping fees, and the platform rewards competitive pricing and strong service metrics with better visibility.

Flipkart Ads and trust programmes like F-Assured lift discovery and buyer confidence, while on-time dispatch and low returns protect your account health and search ranking, especially during big events like Big Billion Days.

Who Flipkart suits

Brands across electronics, home, fashion and general merchandise that want broad reach with India's value-conscious shoppers and are ready to price and fulfil competitively.

Flipkart seller FAQs

How long does it take to launch on Flipkart?

Most brands go live within two to four weeks, covering account setup, verification, catalogue build and creative. We manage the full onboarding so you avoid the common approval delays.

Do you manage Flipkart Ads?

Yes. We plan and run Flipkart advertising alongside pricing and listing optimisation, and report on spend against sales so the budget stays profitable.

How do you protect account health?

We keep dispatch times, cancellations and returns within Flipkart's thresholds, monitor metrics weekly, and act quickly on any policy or listing issues before they affect your ranking.
