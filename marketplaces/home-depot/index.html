How selling on Home Depot works

The Home Depot marketplace connects DIY, hardware, tools and home brands to a huge US home-improvement audience, with strong requirements around product data and delivery for bulky items.

Detailed specifications, accurate attributes and reliable logistics drive discovery and trust with project and trade buyers, who research carefully before committing to a purchase.

Who Home Depot suits

Home-improvement, building-materials, tools and large-format home brands targeting US DIY and professional shoppers.

For Home Depot, we manage rich product data, freight and delivery logistics for bulky items, pricing, order and returns handling, and clear reporting. That gives DIY and professional buyers the specifications and reliability they expect, and keeps your catalogue discoverable in a research-heavy, project-driven marketplace.

Home Depot seller FAQs

What does Home Depot require to sell?

It reviews brand fit, catalogue quality and fulfilment, particularly for bulky and project goods. We prepare and manage the application and onboarding.

How is delivery of large items handled?

Bulky products need dependable logistics. We help set up and manage shipping so delivery is reliable and costs stay sensible.

What catalogue standards apply?

Accurate specifications, complete attributes and clear imagery are essential for DIY and trade buyers. We build the catalogue to meet them.
