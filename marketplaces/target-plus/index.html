Target Plus (Target+) marketplace management

Target Plus is invite-only — Target curates which brands join, which makes it high-trust and less crowded, but harder to get into. EcommOcean helps you position for approval and, once you're in, runs the account: curated assortment, drop-ship operations and listings that fit Target's shopper. It's a slower door to open, and worth it.

What we do on Target+

Approval positioning

Preparing your brand, catalog and pitch to fit Target's curation criteria, and managing the invite process.

Assortment strategy

Selecting the curated assortment that fits Target's shopper and merchandising standards.

Drop-ship operations

Setting up and running the drop-ship (partner-fulfilled) model Target Plus relies on.

Listing optimisation

On-brand, compliant listings with the content quality Target's platform expects.

Order & SLA management

Meeting Target's delivery and service SLAs to stay in good standing.

Growth & reporting

Expanding the assortment as it proves out, with clear monthly reporting.
