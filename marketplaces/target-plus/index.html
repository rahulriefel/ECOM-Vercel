How selling on Target Plus works

Target Plus is Target's invite-only, curated marketplace. You cannot simply sign up — approval and brand fit matter, and the assortment is kept deliberately tight.

It rewards on-brand, high-quality catalogues that match Target's shopper and design sensibility. Presentation, reliability and a clean product range are what get and keep you in.

Who Target Plus suits

Established, on-brand consumer products — home, lifestyle, apparel and more — that fit Target's curated, design-conscious assortment.

Target Plus seller FAQs

How do I get invited to Target Plus?

Invitations depend on brand fit and category need. We help position and prepare your brand for consideration and manage the approval process.

What makes a good Target Plus fit?

On-brand, well-photographed, quality products that match Target's shopper and design standards. We audit your catalogue against those expectations first.

How do you manage operations?

We handle listings, pricing, orders, SLAs and returns, with clear monthly reporting, so your Target Plus presence stays healthy and on-brand.
