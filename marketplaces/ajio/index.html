How selling on AJIO works

AJIO is Reliance's fashion-first platform with a curated, trend-led assortment. It leans on strong presentation and seasonality, so your catalogue and imagery need to match a style-led shopper's expectations.

Commercial terms and arrangements vary by category, and competitive pricing plus a well-built, on-trend catalogue drive visibility. Reliable operations and stock availability keep your products selling through.

Who AJIO suits

AJIO seller FAQs

How long does AJIO onboarding take?

Timelines depend on catalogue readiness and the onboarding arrangement, but most brands are live within a few weeks once imagery and product data are ready. We manage the process end to end.

What catalogue standards does AJIO expect?

AJIO is style-led, so it rewards clean, on-trend imagery and complete, accurate product attributes. We build and optimise the catalogue to fit.

Do you manage pricing and markdowns?

Yes. We set and adjust pricing and participate in events and markdowns strategically, so promotions drive volume without eroding your margin.
