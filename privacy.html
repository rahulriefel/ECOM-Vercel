LAST UPDATED — JULY 2026

This policy explains what information EcommOcean ("we", "us") collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023. Note: this is a starting template — have it reviewed by a legal professional before relying on it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit — name, phone number, email address and the message you send through our contact form or WhatsApp.
  • Technical data — IP address and basic request logs, used for security and rate limiting.

How we use it

  • To send you information you have asked for. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for their marketing.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored on our server and delivered to our business email. Access is limited to the EcommOcean team.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and, where configured, review content from the Google Maps/Places API. Those services may receive your IP address as part of serving those requests. WhatsApp conversations are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to understand aggregate traffic and page performance. These are cookieless, do not track you across sites, and do not collect personally identifiable information.
