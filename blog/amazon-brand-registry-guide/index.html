Amazon Brand Registry: what it unlocks and how to qualify

RMBy Rahul Mishra · 21 September 2026 · 6 min read

If you own a brand and sell on Amazon, Brand Registry is one of the highest-leverage things you can set up. It protects your listings from hijackers and unlocks a set of tools that make your pages convert far better. Here is the short version.

What you unlock

  • A+ content: rich images, comparison charts and brand storytelling on your product pages, which lifts conversion and average order value.
  • Brand Store: a mini-site inside Amazon for your full range.
  • Sponsored Brands: ad formats that show your logo and a custom headline.
  • Brand protection: faster removal of counterfeit or hijacked listings, and more control over your content.
  • Better analytics: search-term and customer insights you do not get otherwise.

What you need to qualify

The core requirement is a registered or pending trademark for your brand in the country you are selling in. Once that is in place, enrolment links your trademark to your seller account. The trademark step is the one that takes time, so start it early — it is often the gate that holds up everything else.

Is it worth it?

For any brand planning to build on Amazon rather than just clear stock, yes. The A+ content and brand protection alone usually justify it.
