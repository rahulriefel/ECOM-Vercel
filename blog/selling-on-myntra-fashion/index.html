Selling on Myntra: what a fashion brand needs to know

RMBy Rahul Mishra · 12 October 2026 · 7 min read

Myntra is where a huge share of India shops for fashion, which makes it a natural channel for apparel, footwear and accessory brands — but it plays by fashion rules, not generic marketplace rules. Here is what matters.

Catalogue quality is everything

Fashion sells on imagery. Myntra expects clean, consistent, high-quality photos — often on models — with multiple angles and detail shots. A weak catalogue will underperform no matter how good the product is. Budget for proper product photography before you list.

Sizing and fit reduce returns

Fit is the number-one reason fashion gets returned. Accurate, product-specific size charts and honest descriptions are your best defence — the same logic we cover in reducing return rates. Getting sizing right protects both your margin and your ratings.

Standing out on the platform

  • Trend-aligned catalogue: stock and merchandise around what is in season.
  • Competitive entry pricing: reviews and rank are hard to earn cold, so plan your launch pricing and promotions.
  • Fast, reliable fulfilment: Myntra buyers expect quick delivery and easy returns.

Is Myntra right for you?

If you are a fashion or lifestyle brand, it belongs in your channel mix — often alongside Amazon and your own store. If your catalogue is not visual-ready, fix that first. Our Myntra management team handles onboarding, cataloguing and growth end to end.
