SEO that compounds

Paid traffic stops the moment you stop paying; SEO keeps working. EcommOcean builds organic search visibility that compounds over time — technical foundations, on-page optimisation and content that earns rankings and, increasingly, citations in AI search — all measured against traffic and revenue, not vanity metrics.

Our approach

We start with an audit — technical health, current rankings, competitors and opportunities — then fix the foundations that hold you back before chasing new keywords. Search rewards sites that are fast, crawlable and genuinely useful.

From there we optimise your key pages, build a content plan around buyer intent, and strengthen internal linking so authority flows to the pages that matter. SEO is a compounding investment, so we work to a plan rather than one-off tweaks.

E-commerce and AI search

For stores, category and product pages are where commercial intent lives, so that is where we focus — unique descriptions, clean structure and product schema that can earn rich results. Thin or duplicate content is the most common thing holding stores back.

Search is also changing: AI answer engines increasingly summarise and cite sources. We write clear, structured, factual content that is easy for those systems to quote, so you are visible in both classic and AI search.

SEO FAQs

How long does SEO take to work?

SEO is a medium-to-long-term investment. Technical fixes can help quickly, but building rankings and traffic typically takes a few months and compounds from there. We are honest about timelines up front.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No reputable SEO can guarantee specific rankings — Google decides those. What we guarantee is sound, white-hat work on the factors that move rankings, and transparent reporting on progress.

Do you do e-commerce SEO?

Yes, it is a core focus. We optimise category and product pages, structured data and site architecture for stores, which is where most buying intent and ranking opportunity sit.

SEO works best on a fast, well-built site — see website development and e-commerce development — and alongside paid marketing. Explore practical guides on our blog.
