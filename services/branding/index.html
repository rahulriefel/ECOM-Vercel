Branding and creative

Branding and creative that stands out

In a crowded market, a strong, consistent brand is what makes buyers choose you and remember you. EcommOcean builds brand identity and creative — from logo and guidelines to product photography, video and marketplace assets — that make your brand unmistakable across every place it appears.

What we do

Brand identity

Logo, colour, type and visual language that capture who you are and set you apart.

Brand guidelines

A clear system so your brand stays consistent across every channel and team.

Product photography

Clean, lifestyle and detail shots that sell online and cut returns.

Video

Short-form and product video for social, ads and storefronts.

Packaging and creative

Packaging, ad creative and marketing assets that carry the brand through.

Marketplace creative

A+ content and listing imagery that lift conversion on Amazon and beyond.

How we build brands

Strong branding starts with clarity — who you are for, what you stand for, and what makes you different. We turn that into a visual identity: logo, colour, typography and a consistent look that feels like you and no one else.

We then package it into guidelines so the brand stays consistent everywhere, and produce the creative you need to use it — photography, video, packaging and campaign assets — so the brand comes to life, not just a logo in a folder.

Creative that sells online

Online, your imagery does the selling. We produce product photography and video that show the product clearly and in context — which lifts conversion and reduces returns, because buyers know exactly what they are getting.

We also build marketplace-specific creative: A+ content, enhanced listings and listing imagery that help you stand out and convert next to bigger brands on Amazon, Flipkart and beyond.

Branding and creative FAQs

What is included in branding?

Typically brand strategy, logo and visual identity, and guidelines. We can extend into photography, video, packaging and campaign creative, scaled to what your brand actually needs.

Do you do product photography?

Yes. We produce clean, lifestyle and detail photography, plus video, built for stores, social and marketplaces — imagery designed to convert, not just look nice.

Can you create marketplace creative like A+ content?

Yes. We design A+ content, enhanced listings and listing imagery for Amazon and other marketplaces, so your brand looks premium and converts on the channels you sell on.

Branding pairs with our UI/UX design, website development and social media services for one coherent brand across every touchpoint.
