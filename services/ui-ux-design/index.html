UI/UX design

UI/UX design that turns visitors into customers

Good design is not decoration — it is the difference between a visitor who converts and one who leaves. EcommOcean runs research-led UI/UX design for web and mobile: interfaces and design systems that look sharp, feel effortless, and guide people toward the action you want.

What we do

User research

Understanding your users and their goals so design decisions are grounded in evidence, not guesswork.

Wireframing and prototyping

Structure and flows mapped and tested before a pixel of visual design is committed.

Design systems

Reusable components and clear guidelines so your product stays consistent as it grows.

Interface design

Clean, on-brand visual design that builds trust and makes the next step obvious.

Usability and CRO

Removing friction and testing changes so more visitors complete the journey.

Developer handoff

Organised, build-ready files and specs so development is smooth and faithful.

Our design process

We start by understanding your users, your goals and the actions that matter, then map the structure and flows as wireframes. Testing the skeleton early is far cheaper than reworking a finished design.

From there we layer on visual design that is on-brand and accessible, package it into a reusable system, and hand it to development build-ready. The result looks great and, more importantly, performs.

Why UI/UX drives revenue

Every extra step, unclear label or slow interaction costs you customers. Thoughtful UX removes that friction, and strong UI builds the trust people need to buy — especially on a store or brand they are meeting for the first time.

Design and conversion are the same goal: make the right action the easy one. We design to that principle and measure the effect, rather than chasing aesthetics for their own sake.

UI/UX design FAQs

What tools do you design in?

We design in Figma, which makes collaboration, prototyping and developer handoff clean and fast. You get organised, shareable files, not a locked black box.

Can you redesign an existing product?

Yes. We often audit and redesign existing sites and apps, improving usability and conversion while keeping what already works. We can do a focused revamp or a full redesign.

Do you hand off cleanly to developers?

Absolutely. We deliver build-ready files, specs and, where useful, a component system, so your developers — or ours — can build exactly what was designed.

Design pairs naturally with our website development and e-commerce development services, and with branding and creative for a fully cohesive brand.
