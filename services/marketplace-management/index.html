Marketplace management

Marketplace management, handled end to end

Selling on a marketplace isn't a one-time listing job — it's a daily operation. EcommOcean runs that operation for you across Amazon, Flipkart, Walmart, Meesho and 12 more channels: we launch your account, build and optimise the catalog, run the advertising, defend account health, and hand you reporting you can actually read. One team, flat monthly pricing, and your accounts stay 100% yours.

What's included

Account setup & verification

New seller or vendor registration, brand registry, category approvals and store setup — done right the first time.

Listings & catalog

Keyword-led titles, bullets and descriptions, A+ / enhanced content, variations and bulk catalog uploads that convert.

Advertising (Sponsored & PPC)

Sponsored Products, Brands and Display managed to a target ACOS/TACoS — structured, bid-tuned and reported weekly.

Marketplace SEO & ranking

Keyword research, listing optimisation and ranking strategy to win organic placement where buyers actually search.

Account health & compliance

Policy monitoring, IP and authenticity flags, case management and reinstatements — we keep the account safe.

Inventory, pricing & reporting

Stock and buy-box monitoring, repricing guidance and a monthly profit report — not vanity metrics.
