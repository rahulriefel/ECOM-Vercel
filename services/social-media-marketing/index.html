Social media marketing

Social media that builds brands

Social media is where brands are discovered, remembered and trusted. EcommOcean runs content, community and paid social that build genuine engagement — not just follower counts — across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and the platforms where your audience actually spends time.

What we do

Content creation

On-brand posts, stories and graphics planned around a calendar, not posted at random.

Short-form video

Reels, Shorts and TikTok-style video — the format driving the most reach today.

Community management

Engaging your audience, answering questions and turning followers into customers.

Paid social

Facebook and Instagram campaigns for reach, engagement and sales, tied to real results.

Influencer marketing

Matching your brand with creators whose audience genuinely fits, and measuring the return.

Analytics

Reporting on what actually drives reach, engagement and revenue, so effort compounds.

How we run social

We start with a clear strategy — who you are talking to, what you stand for, and what you want social to achieve — then build a content calendar around it. Consistency and a distinct voice beat sporadic, generic posting every time.

We create the content, manage the community, and layer paid social where it accelerates results. Everything is measured, so we double down on what works and drop what does not, rather than posting on hope.

Organic and paid together

Organic social builds brand, trust and community over time; paid social buys reach and drives specific actions now. Used together they reinforce each other — content proves the brand, ads scale what resonates.

Short-form video sits at the centre of both, because it is what platforms reward with reach right now. We plan content that works organically and can be amplified with spend when it earns it.

Social media marketing FAQs

Which platforms do you cover?

Primarily Instagram, Facebook and YouTube, plus others like LinkedIn or TikTok where they fit your audience. We focus effort where your customers actually are rather than trying to be everywhere.

Do you create the content yourselves?

Yes. We plan, create and schedule on-brand content — graphics, posts and short-form video — and can work with your product, photography and creators to produce it.

Should I do organic, paid, or both?

Most brands benefit from both: organic builds trust and community, paid scales what works. We usually start with a strong organic foundation and add paid where it earns a return.

Social pairs with our digital marketing and SEO and branding and creative services, and supports your marketplace and store sales.
