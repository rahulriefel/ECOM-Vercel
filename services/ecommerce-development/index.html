E-commerce development

E-commerce development that's built to convert

Your storefront is the one channel you fully own — it shouldn't be the slowest or the hardest to update. EcommOcean designs and builds high-converting online stores on Shopify, WooCommerce or a custom stack, wired to your marketplaces, payments and logistics. Fast, SEO-ready foundations, clean admin, and a checkout that doesn't leak sales.

What we build

Shopify stores

Custom Shopify and Shopify Plus themes, app integrations and conversion-focused product and checkout flows.

WooCommerce stores

WordPress + WooCommerce builds for brands that want full control, custom logic and no platform lock-in.

Custom & headless

Bespoke and headless commerce (Next.js front-ends) when you need performance and a tailored experience.

Integrations

Payments, shipping, ERP/inventory, CRM and marketplace sync so your store and channels stay in step.

Performance & SEO

Core Web Vitals, clean semantic markup, structured data and technical SEO built in from day one.

CRO & analytics

Conversion-focused UX, product-page best practice and analytics so you can see and fix what leaks.
